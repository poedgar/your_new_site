**Why the list is short**
- I checked about 45 businesses in the [zaxid.net catalogue](https://zaxid.net/catalog/) across auto services, furniture makers, dentists and construction. Most either already had a website or listed no email at all; the furniture and dental entries were mostly empty shells.
- [list.in.ua](https://list.in.ua/), the biggest directory, blocks automated access (HTTP 403), so I skipped it rather than work around the block. You can still browse it yourself.
- The directory data looks old, so some of these emails may no longer work. Calling first is safer.
- A business with no website may still have an Instagram or Facebook page. That's often a good opening: "you have customers on Instagram, but Google can't find you".

**Before you email them**
- **No contact person's name:** these are businesses, so the CSV has no person names. Use a greeting that doesn't need `{name}`, such as "Добрий день!", or the email will read "Добрий день, !".
- **Ukrainian law on commercial email:** as I understand it, the Law "On Electronic Commerce" (Article 10) allows commercial emails only with the recipient's consent, so a cold bulk mailing is legally risky. Short individual emails about one specific problem (for example "your shop isn't findable on Google") or a phone call are both safer and more effective. Check this yourself before sending.

To get more leads quickly, Google Maps is the best source. Search a niche and city (for example "СТО Львів") and the listings without a "Website" button are exactly the businesses you want. That's quick to do by hand, but scraping Google Maps breaks its terms, so I didn't automate it. I can import this CSV into your Contacts list, or search other cities or niches through directories that allow access.
